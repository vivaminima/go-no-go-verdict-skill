# Verdict: Invoice reminder script for freelance designers

<!-- A made-up idea with made-up numbers. It shows the shape of a finished verdict, nothing else. -->

## 1. New artifact

Yes. A small command-line script that reads a spreadsheet of invoices and drafts a polite
reminder email for each overdue one. It does not exist yet; today the reminders are written by hand.

## 2. First buyer

Mina, a freelance brand designer I worked next to last year. She told me she chases late
invoices by hand "every single month".

- She sends about 12 invoices a month [observed: her message to me, 2026-10-28].
- About 3 of them go overdue [assumption].

## 3. Channel

- Found through: one post in the freelance designers' forum where I already answer questions.
  The forum has 4,200 members [observed: forum member count page, 2026-11-02].
- Paid through: a hosted checkout page from a payment provider. No store of my own.
- I expect 40 visitors from the first post [assumption].

## 4. Price and fees

- Price: 15 dollars, paid once [assumption].
- Payment provider fee per sale: [to check] on the provider's official fees page before launch.
- Tax handling on digital sales: [to check] with the provider's documentation.

## 5. Time

- First sellable version: 6 h of agent working time [assumption].
- Sales page and checkout setup: 2 h [assumption].

## 6. Blockers to first revenue

- The payment provider account needs identity verification, which only I can do. Their page
  says it takes up to 3 business days [observed: provider onboarding page, 2026-11-02].
- I have no sample spreadsheet from a real freelancer yet. Mina offered hers with the names removed.

## 7. Cap and stop conditions

- Cap: 12 h of work and 0 dollars of ad spend [assumption].
- Stop on 2026-12-15 if fewer than 3 people have paid by then [assumption].
- Stop earlier if the fee check shows that less than half of the price reaches me.

## 8. Verdict

Verdict: Fix-then-go

The idea is small enough to test, but two things are unknown and cheap to find out: the fee,
and whether anyone other than Mina has the problem.

First step: Open the payment provider's fees page and fill in the two open lines of the price section.
